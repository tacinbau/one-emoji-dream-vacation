A make.fun entry for [One Emoji, One Dream Vacation](https://make.fun/bounty/u-grand-elephant-19-one-emoji-one-dream-vacation-62879a35): send exactly one emoji for your dream vacation, and a judge picks the most evocative.

## The entry

🌅 — sunrise over the sea. Sun, sea and horizon at dawn on the beach: the whole postcard in a single glyph, the moment you book the flight for. It is a scene, not an object — a stranger sees the place before reading a word — and a specific one, a dawn, rather than any holiday.

Why this emoji wins the judge's brief, in its own order: it plainly names a dream vacation (beach, sea, faraway morning); it is vivid at a glance; it points at one particular dream; and it reads instantly across cultures and screens, clear at small size.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

Writes exactly one emoji to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (defaults to `/output`), then reads it back and checks the gate: 1–8 characters, not only white space.
